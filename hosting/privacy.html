Privacy policy

Lynda's Rummi Time · last updated 30 October 2026

Lynda's Rummi Time is a tile rummy game. It is played on your own device, and against other people online if you choose to. This policy explains the little data the game handles, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. There is no advertising in the game, nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing, and nothing tracks you across other apps or websites.

If you never play online

Single player and Same computer need no account and send nothing anywhere. The players you register, their pictures, your settings and your statistics stay in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What goes online when you play multiplayer

Multiplayer runs on Google's Firebase Realtime Database, which stores the following for as long as you keep your registration:

  • An account identifier. The app signs in anonymously and is given a random identifier. It is not your Apple ID, your email or your phone number.
  • Your handle — the 3-to-16 character name other players find you by. If you register as a public player, your handle, table name and picture appear in a directory other players can browse. A private player is only reachable by someone who types their exact handle.
  • Your table name and picture. The picture is either an emoji or, if you choose to take one, a photo from the camera. A photo you set is uploaded with your player record so the people you play with can see it.
  • Chat messages, only if you sent any in an earlier version of the game. Chat is currently switched off. Earlier messages are stored with the private game they were sent in and are readable by the players at that table.
  • Games and statistics — the state of games you are in, your results, rankings, who you have recently played with, friends you have added and invitations you have sent or received.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications, so Apple's push service can tell you it is your turn while the game is closed.

Reports and blocks

If you report a player, the report (who you reported, the reason, the time and your account identifier) is stored so that it can be looked at. If you block a player, your list of blocked players is stored with your account and only you can see it. A player who breaks the community rules can have their picture and listing removed.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in with Apple is optional and exists only so you can play as the same person on more than one device. When you use it, Apple gives the game an identifier for your account; the game does not ask Apple for your name or your email address, and does not receive them.

The camera

The camera is used for one thing: taking a profile photo when you register a player, and only at the moment you ask for it. The game does not take photos at any other time.

Contacts, Messages and Mail

You can invite someone by Messages or Mail. The phone number or email address you save for a person is kept on your device only — it is never uploaded. The game does not read your contacts.

Who else sees it

Google, as the operator of Firebase, processes and stores the multiplayer data on our behalf — see the Firebase privacy documentation. Apple handles notification delivery and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple. Both also handle technical data, such as your device's IP address, when your device connects to them. Nobody else receives your data. There is no analytics or advertising code in the game.

Where your data is stored

The multiplayer data is stored on Google's servers in the United States. If you live in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, this means your data is transferred outside your region. Those transfers rely on the safeguards in Google's data processing terms for Firebase, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland

The controller of your data is Tom Rue, who can be reached at the address under Getting in touch. Your data is used on these legal bases:

  • To provide the game you asked to play (performance of a contract): your account identifier, handle, table name, games and statistics.
  • With your consent: a profile photo and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing the photo or turning notifications off, without affecting anything done before then.
  • For a legitimate interest in keeping the game running and secure: the technical data needed to connect your device.

You have the right to ask for access to your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive a copy in a portable form. To use any of these rights, write to the address below. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

You do not have to provide any of this data: it is only needed to play online. The game makes no automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects, and it does not profile you.

How long your data is kept

Your online data is kept until you delete your account, as described under Deleting your data.

Children

The game collects no more from a child than from anyone else. It asks for no personal details beyond a handle, a chosen name and an optional picture. Anyone under 16, or under the lower age set by their country, should ask a parent or guardian before playing online. If you would rather a child shared nothing at all, use Single player or Same computer.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything held on your device: the players you registered, their pictures, your settings and your statistics. It does not remove your online account.

To delete your online account, open Multiplayer › My handle and picture › Delete my account, or ☰ › Delete my account… on the start screen. This removes your players, handles and names, your directory entry, rankings, friends, recent players, invitations received and notification settings, your list of blocked players, and it removes your sign-in. If the sign-in cannot be removed at that moment, the app signs you out and the rest is still deleted.

Games you are playing with other people carry on without you, and an open lobby or finished game you hosted is deleted. A finished game hosted by someone else may still show the name you played under and any earlier chat messages. To have those removed too, or if you no longer have the app, write to the address below. Reports you made about other players are kept while they are needed to keep the game safe.
