Community rules

Lynda's Rummi Time · last updated 30 October 2026

These rules apply to everyone who registers a handle and plays online. By registering you agree to them. They come on top of Apple's standard license agreement for apps. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or for abusive players.

What is not allowed

  • Names, handles or pictures that are offensive, sexual, violent, hateful or threatening, or that pretend to be someone else.
  • Pictures of other people without their permission.
  • Harassing, threatening or bullying other players.
  • Cheating, or interfering with the game or with other people's games.

Filtering, reporting and blocking

  • Offensive names and handles are refused when you register.
  • Press ⋯ next to a player to report them. Every report is looked at within 24 hours.
  • From the same place you can block a player. They are hidden from your lists, their invitations are not shown to you, and random games do not put you together. Undo this under Multiplayer › Blocked players.

What happens when a rule is broken

Objectionable content is removed, and a player who breaks these rules can lose their picture, their public listing or their account. A player reported by several people is hidden until the reports have been looked at.

Getting in touch

To report something, or to ask about a decision: trueathome@gmail.com.
