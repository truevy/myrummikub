Lynda's Rummi Time — support

A tile rummy game for iPhone and iPad, played on your own or online with other people.

Getting in touch

Questions, problems or ideas: trueathome@gmail.com. Please say which device you are on and what happened.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Single player and Same computer work straight away. You only register a handle if you want to play with other people online.
  • How do people find me? By your handle. Public players can also be matched into random games; private players are only reachable by someone who types their exact handle.
  • How long do I have for a turn? In a random game, 24 hours, with a reminder after 20. Private games are not timed.
  • Why am I not getting notifications? Check Settings › Notifications › Rummi Time on your device. While the game is open it shows its own banner instead.
  • Can I play on more than one device? Yes — sign in with Apple so both devices share the same player.
  • How do I report or block someone? Press ⋯ next to the player in All players, in your friends or in a game's summary, or use the link on their invitation. Reports are looked at within 24 hours, or write to the address above. See the community rules.
  • How do I delete my data? See the privacy policy.
